Hi, I'm shadey. This is where I write up what I build, break, and eventually fix in my homelab: virtualisation, Kubernetes, GitOps, networking, storage, and the odd side project.

Each entry covers the problem, the approach, the result, and what I'd do differently. Hostnames, addresses, and credentials are redacted or swapped for placeholders.

### Who I'd like to meet:
People who document their setups, and anyone who has ever said "it's only a quick change" before taking down the cluster.
